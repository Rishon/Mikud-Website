Mikud is a free service for finding Israeli zip codes by address. It is operated by Rishon Jaffe, trading as rishon.systems (the "operator", "we"), who is the controller of any personal information processed through the site. You can reach us at [mail@rishon.systems](mailto:mail@rishon.systems).

This policy explains what information is processed when you use the site, why, where it is kept, who receives it, and the choices and rights you have. You are not legally required to provide any information; entering an address is simply necessary to receive a zip code for it.

## Information processed during a lookup

When you search for a zip code, the address details you enter (city, street, house number and entrance) are sent to our server and passed to Israel Post's zip code service to retrieve the result. This processing is necessary to provide the service you requested.

We do not keep a record of who searched for which address. A zip code result for a given address may be kept in our server's memory for up to six hours so that repeat lookups are faster; this cache contains only the address and its zip code, never who requested it.

City and street suggestions are answered from lists of Israel's settlements and streets published by the government (see Third parties). These lists live on our server and contain no information about you.

## Technical information

Like most websites, our server may keep short-lived technical records such as your IP address, request time and browser type, used only for security and troubleshooting, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and available.

To keep the service available to everyone, the number of requests per IP address is limited. For this purpose IP addresses are kept in the server's memory for no longer than one minute.

## Information stored on your device

Your list of recent zip codes is stored only in your browser and is never sent to us. You can delete any entry with the delete icon, or clear all site data from your browser settings.

The site uses one essential cookie to remember the interface language you chose; it is kept for twelve months and is not used for tracking. Your cookie decision is also stored in your browser so the banner is not shown again.

## Analytics cookies

With your consent in the cookie banner, the site uses two analytics services. Neither is loaded unless you accept, and you can withdraw consent at any time with the button at the bottom of this page.

**Google Analytics** (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) measures how the site is used: pages viewed, interactions, device and browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to distinguish visitors. Google may process this data on servers outside Israel and the EU, including in the United States. Learn more in [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can also install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Microsoft Clarity** (Microsoft Corporation) captures how you use and interact with the site through behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replay, which we use to improve the site. Usage data is captured using first-party and third-party cookies and similar technologies. Clarity masks the text you type into form fields by default, so the addresses you enter are not visible in recordings. Microsoft may also use this data as described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement); see also [Clarity's data privacy page](https://clarity.microsoft.com/privacy). We do not use Microsoft Advertising.

Google and Microsoft retain analytics data according to their own retention policies, which are described in the documents linked above.

## Bot protection

The zip code lookup is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc.), which runs in the background to tell people apart from automated scripts without asking you to solve puzzles. For this purpose Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser characteristics, and may use cookies or similar technologies on its own domain that are strictly necessary for the check. This is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service from abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Third parties

The following parties receive information when you use the site, each under its own privacy policy:

- **Israel Post Company Ltd.** receives the address you look up, in order to return its zip code. See [Israel Post's privacy policy](https://israelpost.co.il/%D7%90%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%93%D7%95%D7%90%D7%A8-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C).
- **Cloudflare** processes technical data for bot protection, as described above.
- **Google** and **Microsoft** receive usage data only if you accept analytics cookies, as described above.

The lists of settlements and streets used for suggestions come from [Israel's open government data portal](https://data.gov.il) and are public data; nothing about you is sent there. We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

## Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority such as the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

In practice, we keep no personal information about you on our server beyond the short-lived technical records described above, so these rights are mostly exercised by clearing the site's data in your browser and by withdrawing analytics consent. For any privacy question or request, email us at [mail@rishon.systems](mailto:mail@rishon.systems).

## Children

The site is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest update appears at the top of the page, and continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
